Notes

Art on the Run was presented at Grace Harkin Gallery, 526 East 11th Street, New York, from March 7–29, 1989, with a reception on Thursday, March 16, from 7–9 p.m.

The show card lists Mark Kostabi, Mike Cockrill, Kevin Larmee, Ron English, Meyer Tannenbaum, Robert Katona, Inez Andrucyk, Fred Duignan, Philippe La Prelle, George Haas, Bryan Perrin, Leslie Tillett, Shawn Eichman, Joyce Hutchins, Ena Kostabi, and Martin Springhetti.

Ron English’s inclusion places the exhibition within the late-1980s East Village network of artists moving between gallery, club, and alternative exhibition spaces.

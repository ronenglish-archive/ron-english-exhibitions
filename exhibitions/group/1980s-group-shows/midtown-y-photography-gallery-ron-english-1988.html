Notes

Surviving Ron English ephemera for this exhibition takes the form of a showcard titled Photographs by Ron English.

Midtown Y Photography Gallery records list Ron English together with Abbot Genser and Jeanine El'Gazi for the May–June 1988 exhibition file, indicating that the presentation was part of a group exhibition rather than a standalone solo show.

A contemporaneous exhibition listing places Jeanine El'Gazi, Ron English, and Abbot Genser at Midtown Y Photography Gallery, 344 East 14th Street, all through June 26.

Sources

  • Midtown Y Photography Gallery Records, 1972–1996, New York Public Library guide, listing “Ron English; Abbot Genser; Jeanine El'Gazi” in the 1988 May–June exhibition file.

Notes

Fetishes: Art for the Ego was presented by English Eichman at B2, 157 Hudson Street, New York, on Wednesday, January 25, 1989.

The showcard lists an exclusive exhibition featuring Andy Warhol, Ed Ruscha, Doris Kloster, Benjamin Leipelt, Daze, Bill Klaila, Mike Bidlo, Cockrill/Judge Hughes, Lady Pink, Cliff Leigh, Maggie Ens, Jack Forsell, Anne Greenwell, Christine Dawson, Adam Savage, Alexander Moss, Eric Monder, Marque Cornblatt, Charles McGrady, Ron English, and Shawn Eichman.

The event opened at 10 PM and included complimentary Absolut cocktails from 10 PM to 11 PM, with admission listed as $5 with invitation and $10 without invitation. The showcard gives special thanks to FAD Magazine.

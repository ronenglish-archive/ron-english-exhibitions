Notes

Dubious Edge was a group exhibition held at Theatre Gallery in Dallas from August 3 through August 30, 1985. Contemporary Dallas Observer coverage identified Ron English among the artists presenting photographic work.

A gallery announcement for the exhibition survives in the Paul Rogers Harris Collection of Dallas and Texas Gallery Announcements at the Dallas Museum of Art Archives. A later museum blog caption dated the announcement approximately to 1987; the contemporary dated listing supports the 1985 exhibition date used here.

Notes

The printed showcard announces the gala opening of Power Pathos at Station Museum of Contemporary Art, Houston, on Saturday, July 8, 2006 at 7:00 PM.

The exhibition was on view from June 21 through September 24, 2006 and included Ron English, Daniel Johnston, Gibby Haynes, Clark Fox, and Anthony Ausgang.

The showcard identifies the reproduced image as Ron English, Camo Clown Boy (detail), 2006, oil on canvas.

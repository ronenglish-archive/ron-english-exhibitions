Notes

This catalogue entry records a Palm Springs presentation of 100 Artists See Satan at M Modern Gallery from October 30 through November 14, 2004, with an opening reception on October 30 from 8 to 11 p.m.

The project originated earlier in 2004 at Grand Central Art Center in Santa Ana, where it was organized by curator Mike McGee in response to 100 Artists See God. The published catalogue lists Ron English among the participating artists.

Documentation note: The Palm Springs venue and dates are retained from the catalogue project’s archival record. Independent online confirmation of this specific M Modern presentation was not located during the August 2026 review. The images shown here are publication or catalogue documentation and should not be interpreted as installation views unless further evidence is found.

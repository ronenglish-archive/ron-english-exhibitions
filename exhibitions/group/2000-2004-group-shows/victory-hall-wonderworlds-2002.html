Notes

The printed showcard documents Wonderworlds as a New Art/New Music presentation at Victory Hall in Jersey City, New Jersey, held on Saturdays, June 29 and July 13, 2002, from 7:00 to 11:00 PM.

Ron English is listed among the participating artists. The showcard also announces music by Argama and friends, with concerts at 8:00 PM both nights.

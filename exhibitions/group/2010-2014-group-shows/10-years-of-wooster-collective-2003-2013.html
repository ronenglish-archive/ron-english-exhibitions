Notes

10 Years of Wooster Collective: 2003–2013 celebrated the tenth anniversary of Wooster Collective, the street-art platform founded by Sara and Marc Schiller.

The pop-up exhibition brought together work by more than fifty artists associated with the platform. Contemporary announcements specifically list Ron English among the participating artists.

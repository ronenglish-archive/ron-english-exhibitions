Notes

The surviving exhibition card documents a March 1989 Ron English exhibition at OK Harris Works of Art.

The OK Harris exhibition schedule for 1988–1989 lists Ron English under “Photographs” for March 4–25, 1989.

Together, the card and the gallery schedule identify this as a documented solo exhibition of photographs at OK Harris Works of Art in New York.

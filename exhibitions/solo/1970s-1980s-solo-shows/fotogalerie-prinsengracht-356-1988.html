Ron English

Fotogalerie Prinsengracht 356, Amsterdam, Netherlands.

Front of exhibition showcard for Ron English, Fotogalerie Prinsengracht 356, Amsterdam.
Reverse of exhibition showcard for Ron English, Fotogalerie Prinsengracht 356, Amsterdam.

English translation of Dutch text on reverse

For the American Ron English, there are two attractive aspects to the medium of photography: the possibility of using it to record reality and the possibility of creating illusions. Each of his photographs is the result of using both possibilities at the same time. Looking at his photographs, a three-dimensional scene presented two-dimensionally can sometimes make you dizzy.

Ron English uses no darkroom tricks; he photographs “straight.”

Notes

The invitation card identifies Agit-Pop America as an exhibition of new works by Ron English at Gallery Stendhal, running from April 3 to April 30, 1997.

The same card invites viewers to a cocktail reception on Thursday, April 3, from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m., and gives the gallery address as 386 West Broadway, 2nd floor, New York, NY 10012.

A related after-party flyer documents an official opening-night event at Opera, 539 W 21st St., with complimentary admission and drinks from 10 to 11, hosted by Reuben Rubin and Lee Klein.

A printed promotional piece for the exhibition reproduces Culture Wars and identifies it as a 1996 oil on canvas measuring 40 × 90 in.

A review spread titled Agit-Pop America credits Tarssa Yazdani and lists New York, USA, but the publication title and date are not established here.

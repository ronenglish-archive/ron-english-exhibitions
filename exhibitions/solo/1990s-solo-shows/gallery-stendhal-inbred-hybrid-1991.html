Notes

Inbred/Hybrid was presented at Gallery Stendhal in New York from September 14 through October 13, 1991.

A contemporary feature in HYPE Magazine discussed the exhibition in relation to English’s use of layered pop-cultural imagery and noted his practice of painting over signed or numbered prints by artists including Haring, Kostabi, and Warhol.

The works shown here are confirmed as part of the exhibition, though the full checklist may have included additional paintings.

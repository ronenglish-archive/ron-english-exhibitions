  • Color Corrected — reproduced on the front of the exhibition card.
  • 10,000 Surrealists — illustrated in Where Washington, December 1994.

Notes

Ron English presented Revisionist Modernism in Washington, D.C., in late 1994 and early 1995. The surviving exhibition card documents the show visually and identifies Color Corrected as the painting reproduced on its front.

Contemporary coverage in Where Washington, December 1994, describes the exhibition as a group of irreverent reworkings of twentieth-century art icons and clichés. That notice specifically mentions 10,000 Surrealists and frames the show as a broad homage to figures including Picasso, Frida Kahlo, and Norman Rockwell.

The Where Washington notice also gives additional opening information, stating that the exhibition opened with a reception on December 1 from 6:00 to 9:00 p.m. at 1054 31st Street NW in Georgetown. This contemporary address note helps clarify the Washington presentation alongside other sources that associate the show with the Museum of Contemporary Art.

In period coverage, the exhibition is sometimes described with slightly different wording, including the phrase “Revisionist Art History,” but the sources clearly point to the same Washington showing at the end of 1994.

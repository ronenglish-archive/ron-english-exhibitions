Notes

The Georgetowner describes the exhibition as Clark & Company’s showcase of oil paintings by New York Pop artist Ron English and identifies it as his inaugural exhibition in the Washington area.

The same notice states that the work had a social and political edge, describes English as known for billboard “counter statements,” and says the paintings at Clark & Company “may not be as monumental, but they pack just as big a punch.”

The publication states that the show remained on view until December 17 and lists gallery hours as 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Tuesday through Saturday.

Elsewhere on the same page, a reproduced image is captioned “M&M by Ron English at Galleries 1054—Clark; this is a photo of the painting Marilyn Mickey Coverup.”

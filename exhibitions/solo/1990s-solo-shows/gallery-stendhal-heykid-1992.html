Notes

The exhibition showcard announces HEY! My Kid Could Do That! as a Ron English exhibition of new work at Gallery Stendhal, running from November 12 to December 12, 1992, with an opening reception on November 12 from 6:30 to 9:00 p.m.

The same card notes that all art purchases included a donation to the Learning Through Art / Guggenheim Children’s Program and lists the exhibition as co-sponsored by QP / Ian Alterman.

An Absolute Collection article from October 1992 describes English’s fall New York show as My Kid Could Do That and says it featured collaborations with his two nephews and a niece, as well as collaborations using early childhood works by his wife Tarsa and her two brothers.

The Absolute Collection article also emphasizes that Batmen and dinosaurs recur throughout the series, matching the subjects reflected in the documented exhibition image files.

A January 1993 notice in Spy refers to Gallery Stendhal’s recent exhibit of new works by Ron English, calling it Hey! My Kid Could Do That! and describing it as paintings and drawings created by preschool children and “remastered” by English.

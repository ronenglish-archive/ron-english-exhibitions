Notes

The surviving card advertises New Giclee Canvases by Ron English at Den of Thieves in New York on Thursday, April 11, 1996, at 10 p.m.

The same card states that the event was presented by Camel & Den of Thieves and describes Ron English as an “Agit-Pop Prophet.”

It also includes production information for the canvas editions, naming Canvas Imaging at 118 East 59th Street, Suite 403, New York, N.Y. 10022, with telephone number (212) 980-2984.
